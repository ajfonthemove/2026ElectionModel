Presidential reference sources

Comparisons show the current reported Democratic-minus-Republican margin minus the archived presidential margin, using each source’s published total. They are descriptive observations across different offices and electorates, not forecasts or estimates of the remaining vote.

State references use CNN’s public 2024 and 2020 statewide presidential snapshots. County references use Tony McGovern’s public compilation, distributed under the MIT license. Its 2024 source is Fox News; 2020 sources are Fox News, Politico and The New York Times. The compiler describes the results as nonauthoritative.

These are archived reported counts. Certified-final status and coverage of all valid write-ins have not been independently verified. “Other” is the source total minus Democratic and Republican votes: a nonmajor/unassigned residual, not an invented candidate roster. No two-party renormalization is applied. State and county snapshots are independent; disagreements are retained in the manifest and dataset coverage diagnostics.

County comparisons require an exact five-digit county identifier. Alaska’s election districts and DC’s wards are excluded from county references. Connecticut’s 2024 planning regions retain their distinct identifiers from the legacy counties in 2020. A shared identifier is not a full boundary audit; no historical vote is reallocated to House districts or municipalities. Missing or incompatible geography remains unavailable.

Raw source snapshots were captured November 4, 2026. The manifest records the retrieval timestamps and SHA-256 hashes. CNN attribution identifies the factual source; no license for CNN’s software, branding or other content is asserted.
