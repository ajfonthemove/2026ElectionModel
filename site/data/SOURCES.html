Map sources & attribution

Election Desk renders its own interactive maps from the following preserved geographic sources.

  • State boundaries and nationwide county reference boundaries: US Atlas, derived from U.S. Census Bureau geography. Alaska and Hawaii appear as insets. US Atlas license · source documentation.
  • 2026 House district outlines and California, North Carolina and Kentucky county outlines: the public AP election map. Source URLs, extraction checks and observed map versions are retained in the adjacent House and county manifests.

Nationwide county reference shapes use Census 2017 geography where a 2026 preset is unavailable. Results join exact source FIPS identifiers; unmatched geography stays gray, with all available source rows accessible through tiles.

Reported vote counts and source race calls are provided by CNN. County and statewide feeds can be different snapshots; county totals are never added to the statewide result.
