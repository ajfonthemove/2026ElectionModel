Map sources & attribution

Election Desk renders its own interactive maps from the following preserved geographic sources.

  • State boundaries and nationwide county reference boundaries: US Atlas, derived from U.S. Census Bureau geography. Alaska and Hawaii appear as insets. US Atlas license · source documentation.
  • 2026 House district outlines and California, North Carolina and Kentucky county outlines: the public AP election map. Source URLs, extraction checks and observed map versions are retained in the adjacent House and county manifests.
  • Massachusetts municipal outlines: the WBUR 2026 map reuses its published 2024 town reference. All 351 town identifiers and county assignments match the MassGIS municipality table. Town boundary source manifest.

Nationwide county reference shapes use Census 2017 geography where a 2026 preset is unavailable. Results join exact source FIPS identifiers; unmatched geography stays gray, with all available source rows accessible through tiles.

Reported vote counts and source race calls are provided by CNN. County and statewide feeds can be different snapshots; county totals are never added to the statewide result.

Massachusetts ballot-question counts: AP via WBUR. Governor town results retain every candidate in the CNN feed; the Massachusetts panel sums its verified 351 town records. Ballot and Governor comparisons retain separate source timestamps. Precincts reporting are not an estimate of votes remaining.

CNN’s reporting percentage is its estimated share of votes counted. The displayed remaining percentage is its complement; no absolute remaining-vote count is supplied.

2020/2024 presidential reference sources, licenses and geography. Comparison colors use the selected count ratio or reported margin/share difference. These are descriptive comparisons. House district votes are never allocated from whole counties.

Massachusetts town presidential references use official Secretary of the Commonwealth PD43+ municipal returns for 2024 and 2020. All candidate votes and All Others are included; blanks are recorded separately. Municipal presidential source manifest.
